Intro to robustness
• Deals with deviations from ideal models and their dangers for corresponding inference procedures
• Goal is to develop procedures that are still reliable and reasonably
efficient under small deviations from the model (e.g., an -neighborhood
of the assumed model)
Outlier rejection?
• Might consider a two-step procedure which first “cleans” data, then
applies classical estimation procedure
• However, outliers may be difficult to recognize without an initial (somewhat) robust estimator

1

• Multiple outliers may “mask” each other so that none are rejected
• False rejections/false retentions may cause cleaned data to deviate from
normal assumptions, too
Robustness desiderata
• Efficiency: Should have nearly(?) optimal efficiency under uncontaminated distribution
• Stability: Small deviations from uncontaminated distribution should
only alter performance slightly
• Breakdown: Larger deviations from model should not be catastrophic
