The prose built on it at paper.tex:485 ("Both full BNN and VBLL transformations significantly improved robustness for both NN-α and NN-β") is therefore false as written. A claimed significant effect that is not significant is the single most damaging item here.
2. **`tab:anova_decomposition` heteroscedastic robustness row (paper.tex:399) is wrong by up to 36.4 percentage points and bolds the wrong dominant factor.** Paper 37.0/4.5/17.5/41.0 vs CSV 13.9915/0.6829/7.9502/77.3754 (`table1_anova_summary.csv:5`). The paper says Model dominates; the data says Residual dominates. This also contradicts the paper's own sentence at :380.
3. **The entire robustness half of `tab:anova_decomposition` (24 cells, paper.tex:396–401) is pre-fix output** — every cell differs from `table1_anova_summary.csv`, with Quantile Residual off by 13.3 pp and Quantile Model off by 11.1 pp. Table and prose (:380) now state mutually contradictory numbers for the same quantity.
